How it works

Three steps to workflow nirvana

From signup to fully automated in under five minutes.

01

Connect your stack

Link your existing tools — Slack, GitHub, Jira, Salesforce, and 200 more — with one-click OAuth. No API keys, no configuration files.

02

Describe your workflow

Tell Nexora what you want in plain English. "When a deal closes in Salesforce, notify the team on Slack and create a Jira ticket." That's it.

03

Let AI optimize

Nexora runs your workflow, learns from the results, and continuously suggests improvements. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.
